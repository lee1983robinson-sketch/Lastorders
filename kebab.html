HOW TO PLAY

  • Steer with the arrow buttons (or ← → keys). The scooter speeds up on its own. Brake if it gets hairy.
  • Follow the green beam to the next customer. The distance is at the top.
  • Hold THROW to aim. A dotted arc shows where the kebab will land and the power swings back and forth.
  • Let go when the landing ring sits on the glowing doormat. It's all down to your timing, lane and power.
  • Doormat = Perfect, doorstep = Good, garden = OK. Anything else is a miss, so have another go before you pass the house.
  • Food cools as you ride. Hotter food = bigger tips.
  • Dodge cars, buses, cyclists, bins, roadworks and people on zebra crossings. Three crashes and the scooter's knackered.
  • Squeeze past traffic for near-miss points. Chain deliveries to build your multiplier.
