HOW TO PLAY

  • Orders pop up on your phone. Tap Accept (or Decline if it's too far).
  • Follow the green arrows and the minimap to the takeaway and stop in the yellow ring to collect.
  • Then ride to the customer. Steer with the arrows and slide the speed lever up to go faster, down to slow or stop.
  • When the throw button lights up, hold it to aim and let go when the ring sits on the doormat. Perfect throws get the biggest tips.
  • Hot food and quick deliveries earn more. Squeeze past traffic for near-miss combos.
  • Three crashes and the scooter's knackered. Earn as much as you can in a 5-minute shift.
