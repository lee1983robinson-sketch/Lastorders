    ROUND ON THE HOUSE

    My pubs

    Own every pub in a colour to build. Upgrades boost the round everyone has to buy when they land on you.

    HOW TO PLAY

    • Roll and move round the high street. Pass First Round to collect £200 wages.
    • Buy pubs you land on. When someone lands on yours, they buy you a round (pay rent).
    • Own a whole colour and the round doubles. Then build a Beer garden, Big screen, Function room, Late licence and finally a Superpub.
    • Taxi ranks earn more the more you own. The Brewery and Jukebox charge 4× the dice, or 10× if you own both.
    • Doubles roll again. Three doubles in a row and you're off to The Cells.
    • The Cells: pay £50, use a Bail card, or try for doubles (three goes).
    • Taxes and fines go into the Taxi Kitty. Land on Free Taxi Home to scoop it.
    • Short of cash? Sell upgrades or mortgage pubs in My pubs. Can't pay? You're bust.
    • Winning: last landlord standing, or in a timed game, the richest at closing time (cash, pubs and upgrades).
