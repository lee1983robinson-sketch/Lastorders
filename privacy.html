Privacy policy

Last Orders by Robbo's Game Lab · Last updated 14 November 2026

In short: no ads, no tracking and no accounts. Your scores and settings live on your own phone. If you use the leaderboards, we store a nickname you choose, a random player number and your scores. That's all.

What stays on your phone

Your best scores, game progress, favourites, achievements, play counts and settings (like sound on or off) are saved in your browser's storage on your device. They're never sent to us, except for the scores described below. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling deletes them.

What the leaderboards store

  • A random player number, made up on your phone the first time you play. It isn't linked to your name, phone number, email or Google account.
  • The nickname you pick for the leaderboards. Please don't use your real full name.
  • Your scores for each game, today and all time.

Leaderboard nicknames and scores can be seen by anyone who plays. They're stored with our database provider (Upstash) and our hosting provider (Render) so the leaderboards work.

What we don't collect

No location, contacts, photos, advertising ID, payment details or analytics. We don't sell or share data with advertisers. Your internet address is used for a moment to stop spam on the leaderboards and isn't saved.

Fonts

The app loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so your device asks Google's servers for them, as with most websites. See Google's privacy policy.

The fruit machines

Club Fruitie and Golden Pints are free play only. Credits have no cash value, can't be bought and can't be won as money or prizes.

Age

Last Orders is for adults aged 18 and over. It isn't meant for children and we don't knowingly collect anything from anyone under 18.

Deleting your leaderboard entries

Email the address on our Google Play listing with your nickname and we'll remove your nickname and scores. To delete everything on your phone, clear the app's storage or uninstall it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
